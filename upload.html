To fix · ត្រូវកែ

Reads one document per scanned order (about 4,000 for a full day). A finished day is saved as a summary, so opening it again costs 1 read.

Same words · ពាក្យដូចគ្នា

Colour/size words that mean the same thing in PokPok and Vrich. One group per line, separated by =, e.g. ស = សរ. Phones use the list at once; the "Orders that can't scan" list is re-checked.

Staff & supervisors

Staff names appear as a list on the phones. A supervisor PIN approves one item in one order when the catalog cannot be fixed straight away. Use 6 digits or more.
